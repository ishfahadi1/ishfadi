Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before launch.

Will I need to register again after launch?
No. Your account has already been reserved. When ISHFADI officially launches, simply sign in using the same email and password you created today. Please keep your login credentials safe.
Can I have multiple roles?
Yes. ISHFADI allows one account to be a buyer, host and agent at the same time — any combination.
When will verification happen?
Email verification happens now, right after reservation. Identity verification for hosts, companies and agents takes place after launch inside your dashboard — you will upload the required documents for review by the ISHFADI administration.
Can I edit my information later?
Yes. Once the platform launches, you'll be able to update your profile from your dashboard at any time.
How will I know when ISHFADI launches?
You'll receive an email announcement at the address you reserved with, and our social channels will go live the same day. If you've installed ISHFADI on your phone, you'll be able to open it directly from your home screen.
Can companies register?
Yes. Select "Become a Host" and choose "Company", then enter your company name. You will upload company registration documents after launch, inside the host dashboard.
